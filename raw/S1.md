## 原文摘錄（英文原句，只留和本 Wiki 有關的段落）

Fast fashion is the constant provision of new styles at very low prices.

Textile consumption in the EU increased from 17 kg per person in 2019 to 19 kg per person on average in 2022

around 12 kg of clothing per person is dumped each year in the EU.

To make a single cotton t-shirt, 2,700 litres of fresh water are required

In 2022, textile consumption per person in the EU required on average: 323 square metres of land; 12 cubic metres of water; 523 kg of raw materials.

The textile sector was the third largest source of water degradation and land use in 2020

Textile production is estimated to be responsible for about 20% of global clean water pollution

A single laundry load of polyester clothes can discharge 700,000 microplastic fibres

accumulation of more than half a million tonnes of microplastics on the bottom of the oceans every year

textile purchases in the EU in 2022 generated about 355 kg of CO2 emissions per person

Between 4% and 9% of all textile products put on the European market are destroyed

Used clothes can be exported outside the EU but are mostly (87%) incinerated or landfilled.

Less than half of used garments are collected for reuse or recycling

only 1% of used clothes are recycled into new clothes

The Commission's March 2022 strategy aims to "make textiles more durable, repairable, reusable and recyclable."

Under this text, EU countries are required since January 2025 to collect textiles separately for re-use and recycling.

As from 2026, the destruction of unsold clothes, shoes and accessories will be banned in the EU.

In 2024, MEPs adopted a law forbidding generic environmental claims on products without proof.
